⚡ Quick Tips for Your Setup
  • Use Leads > + Add Lead > Use Lead Capture Form > Grocery Order Entry to initiate these orders
  • Look to your dashboard for the specific workflow items (emails & documents) that are due to send each person.
  • Look to your pipelines to see the bird's eye view of all leads & clients (navigate between pipelines in the top right).
  • When entering someone's project, look 1st at the "What's coming up in the next 7 days" section, and click into your workflow to see what steps have completed and what's ahead.
  • For donations, either skip over the billing workflow entirely and go to fulfillment workflow instead. Or, use the billing workflow with a $0 invoice and "mark items as complete" as needed to get to fulfillment workflow.

Workflows

Every workflow Nicole built for your 17hats account. Click any item to see how it works.

🛒 Grocer Order
Lead Capture Form: Grocery Order Entry
Enter contact's name, location (from custom field drop-down list of locations), date and theme to begin the process.

  • 1Grocer Order will become the project name
  • 2Lands on Availability Request calendar
  • 3Starts 🟣Grocer Order workflow
  • 4Sends to Grocer Orders client pipeline
  • 5No automatic reply is sent to client
🟣Grocer Order workflow

Includes everything from quote to archive.

  • 1Starts with quote template (with Service Agreement - Long term installations/Retail).
  • 2Upon signing of contract, project moves to Float Scheduled Installation calendar & across the Grocer Orders pipeline
  • 3Reminder to add billing person as related contact (on the lower right of the project screen) & submit invoice
  • 4Automated email 5 days before event date: "Your Balloon Installation is Coming Up! (Grocer)"
  • 51 day after event date, "We hope you loved the balloons! (Grocer)" template is prompted to send
  • 6after checking the to-do to confirm payment has been received; auto-archives project
Grocer Orders Pipeline

Groups orders by status.

  • 1Pending: clients land here upon completion of the lead capture form.
  • 2Quote Sent: clients land here upon sending the quote & contract
  • 3Confirmed: clients land here upon signing the contract
  • 4Invoice Submitted: clients land here after you add the related contact, send the invoice and mark the to-do as complete
  • 5Pipeline completion: when to-do marked complete after payment received

💾 Save the Date
Full process:

Includes invoice, calendar update, check-in reminders and option to start 🟣 2 - Billing workflow

  • 1NOTE: This workflow can be started manually, OR by applying the "save the date" project tag
  • 2Send Book Your Date invoice template
  • 3Upon full invoice payment: "date saved" tag is applied (pausing the 🟣 1 - Inquiry (Install) workflow), project moves to Float Scheduled Installation calendar, project lands in Date Saved phase of Client Confirmed Jobs pipeline
  • 42 reminders to check in on Date Saved, 7 days apart
  • 5Ability to move into 🟣 2 - Billing workflow when needed
🔁 Potential Rebooking
Full process:

Starts after using the [INTERNAL] Rebooking Form lead capture form; includes reach out emails 60 & 30 days before the anniversary date of an event.

  • 1Project lands in Potential Rebooking client pipeline upon filling out [INTERNAL] Rebooking Form lead capture form
  • 260 days before anniversary date of the event, project moves to Send Reach Out 1 phase of Potential Rebooking client pipeline + "repeat reach out 1" email template is prompted
  • 3Upon sending the email above, project moves to Contacted phase of the Potential Rebooking client pipeline
  • 430 days before anniversary date of the event, project moves to Send Reach Out 2 phase of Potential Rebooking client pipeline + "repeat reach out 2" email template is prompted
  • 5Upon sending the email above, the Potential Rebooking workflow / project is archived automatically
❌ Unavailable
Full process:

Email to customer, calendar update & auto-archive

  • 1NOTE: This workflow can be started manually, or by applying the "unavailable" project tag
  • 2Immediately prompts "We're Unavailable" email template to approve and send
  • 3Reminder to follow up for future bookings
  • 4Automatic calendar update, removing the project from the Availability Request calendar
  • 5Automatic archive
