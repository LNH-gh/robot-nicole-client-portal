📋 Your Account at a Glance

Nicole set up your 17hats account in April 2026 with a full process for Event Decor inquiries, billing, fulfillment and rebookings.

Since decisions on the products/services and payment schedule/methods were not provided, the products & services list was left as is; and a default payment schedule of 50% due on receipt + remainder due 14 days before the event was applied to the Event Decor Quote template in the 2 - Event Decor Billing workflow.

No automated document reminders were set due to not hearing back about the current outstanding quotes and invoices.

⚡ Quick Tips for Your Setup
  • ✦ Don't forget to set your project time to match your setup time.
  • ✦ Start with your dashboard every day to see what's due for each person, and utilize the workflow templates that are cued up for you so that the workflow automations can run.
  • ✦ Your pipelines will let you see where all your leads and clients are in the process.
  • ✦ You can update your products & services list with bulk delete and bulk upload through Account Settings > Products & Services (under Money Matters). There is a bulk upload csv template towards the top of that page.

    I recommend utilizing the Internal [alphabetical] name for searching, and a customer-friendly name for the Display name. Example: "Arch - Classic - 20ft" internal name becomes "20ft Classic Balloon Arch" display name; and "Column - Organic" internal name becomes "Organic Balloon Column" display name. Due to the alphabetical nature, this can help you find things faster when you start typing in the internal name field.

    Don't worry about adding a workflow to any single product or service; they're set up to run regardless of product/service being billed.
