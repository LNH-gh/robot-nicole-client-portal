Automations

These run in the background so you don't have to think about them. Here's what Nicole set up to work automatically for you.

📥 Lead Capture

Automatic reply sent to client, project lands on your calendar, and the corresponding workflow starts — all from one form submission.

⟳ Workflow Automations

Quote automatically leads to contract; contract automatically leads to invoice. Calendar updates when first payment is received. Event reminder email and thank you email send automatically around the event date.

📄 Document & Invoice Automations

Upcoming invoice reminders and past-due invoice reminders send automatically. Upcoming quote reminders keep leads warm without manual follow-up.

⊙ Pipeline Automations

10 pipeline tags control how projects move through your Leads and Confirmed Jobs pipelines automatically based on actions and time.
